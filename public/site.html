What do I get when I create an agent?

A persistent workspace with a brief, memory, sources, budgets and deliverables. The local preview also launches a token, trading pool, locked liquidity and operating treasury on Anvil using development ETH.

Is Veyl live on Ethereum mainnet?

Ethereum mainnet is the production target. This preview runs real v4 contracts locally. No Veyl market has been deployed on mainnet, and no paid zkAPI run has been performed. See launch readiness.

Who controls the agent’s funds?

The project owner controls the operating treasury and can withdraw it. Operator payments are limited to approved recipients and a daily cap. The market’s liquidity is held separately in a permanent vault with no withdrawal path. Read the contract boundaries.
